# GettaShower Business Concept (Demand-First Rewrite)

## 1) Core Mission
GettaShower helps people find and access nearby showers in real time, especially in:
- crowded cities,
- high-tourism zones,
- high-temperature hotspots.

The product vision is urban freedom: move through the city and always know where you can refresh safely and fast.

## 2) Problem
Millions of people in cities and travel corridors need a shower during the day, but availability is fragmented and hard to discover.

Typical pain points:
- no simple way to find trusted showers nearby,
- uncertain access rules and pricing,
- time lost searching in heat or between activities,
- low visibility for hosts that have underused shower capacity.

This affects city dwellers (commuters, gig workers, runners, cyclists, outdoor workers) and tourists who are mobile all day.

## 3) Solution
GettaShower is a two-sided urban shower network.

### Demand side (users)
- Discover nearby showers on map/list in seconds
- Access trusted partner locations without manual negotiation
- Transparent pricing and seamless digital payment
- Fast flow for "need shower now" situations

### Supply side (hosts)
- Monetize underused shower infrastructure (gyms, hostels, sports facilities, hotels)
- Get session tracking and revenue visibility by location
- Operate with simple digital access and audit trail

Core idea: connect urgent urban demand with existing shower supply, without needing new physical infrastructure.

## 4) Primary Users and Use Cases
1. City dwellers in dense areas:
   - post-commute refresh,
   - pre/post gym or sport,
   - hot-day recovery.
2. Tourists and short-stay visitors:
   - before check-in, after check-out,
   - between transport legs,
   - after long walks in hot cities.
3. Event and mobility users:
   - festival/sport-event attendees,
   - long-distance bus/train travelers.

## 5) Why Now (Market Signals)

### A) Tourism demand is already massive
- UN Tourism reports approximately 1.4 billion international arrivals globally in 2024.
- Europe alone recorded 747 million international arrivals in 2024.
- Eurostat reports EU tourist accommodation nights exceeded 3.0 billion in 2024 (record year).

Implication: the flow of people needing "between-activities" hygiene access is structurally large.

### B) Urban concentration keeps increasing
- World Bank indicator shows around 57.7% of world population is urban (2024).
- UN urbanization reporting confirms long-term growth of city-based populations.

Implication: denser cities create stronger demand for nearby, on-demand utility services.

### C) Heat stress creates a stronger refresh need
- Copernicus reports 2024 as Europe's warmest year on record.
- Summer 2024 was Europe's warmest summer on record.
- Southeastern Europe saw record strong heat-stress days (66 days vs average 29).

Implication: heat and longer warm periods increase the practical value of finding quick shower access.

### D) User behavior supports mobile checkout
- ECB (2024): 55% of euro area consumers prefer cashless payments in-store.
- ECB (2024): 89% of POS transactions were completed or could be completed with non-cash methods.
- Barclays (UK, 2024): 94.6% of eligible in-store card transactions were contactless.

Implication: users are ready for instant, phone-first access and payment.

## 6) Business Model
Demand-first monetization with host-aligned economics:
1. User pays per session (transparent price, e.g. around EUR 6-12 depending on market and host).
2. Revenue split per completed session (host majority share; platform take rate; optional CAC/partner channel share).
3. Optional host SaaS fee for advanced analytics/compliance at scale.

Example split model (illustrative):
- Host: 70%
- GettaShower platform: 20%
- CAC/partner channel: 10%

## 7) Unit Economics (Illustrative, Demand-Led)
City-level session value:

Annual platform revenue = total paid sessions x avg shower price x platform take rate

Illustrative hotspot pilot:
- 10 million seasonal visitors across selected pilot cities,
- 0.5% to 0.8% convert once/year,
- average shower price EUR 10,
- platform take rate 20%.

Result:
- sessions: 50,000 to 80,000,
- GMV: EUR 500,000 to EUR 800,000,
- GettaShower revenue: EUR 100,000 to EUR 160,000.

Host-side impact from same flow:
- host share at 70% = EUR 350,000 to EUR 560,000 distributed across participating venues.

These are conservative pilot assumptions and can scale with:
- repeat usage,
- broader city coverage,
- higher conversion in heat and peak tourism windows.

## 8) Go-to-Market (Hotspot City Strategy)
Phase 1: launch in high-density, high-tourism, high-temperature corridors.
- Initial focus: Barcelona + Milan pilot logic.
- Expand to Madrid, Rome, Istanbul, Paris, Munich, Berlin once repeatable unit economics are proven.

Execution sequence:
1. Supply onboarding around tourist and mobility nodes.
2. Demand capture in moments of acute need (heat, check-in/out gaps, event peaks).
3. Optimize conversion with trust signals: quality, hygiene, and speed.
4. Expand city by city with cluster rollout.

## 9) Strategic Advantage
- Solves an immediate "now" problem, not a low-frequency planning task.
- Uses existing venue infrastructure (capital-light expansion).
- Builds local network effects: more hosts -> better coverage -> more users -> more host revenue.
- Creates operational data moat by location, time, demand spikes, and repeat behavior.

## 10) Risks and Mitigations
- Supply density risk -> start with high-footfall micro-zones, then expand radius.
- Price sensitivity -> dynamic price bands and occasional demand-triggered promotions.
- Trust and quality consistency -> host standards, verified listings, and feedback loops.
- Seasonal demand concentration -> expand use cases for city dwellers to balance off-season volume.

## 11) 12-18 Month Success Metrics
- Coverage: active showers within walking distance in target districts.
- Demand: first-session conversion and repeat rate.
- Economics: session margin, CAC payback, and host retention.
- Service quality: completion rate, satisfaction score, and issue resolution time.
